Igama losapho elakhelwe kwinkonzo

Ilifa lokunyamekela ukusukela ngo-1955.

Njengendlu yosapho esekwe eKapa, siqondisa iintsapho ngobubele iminyaka engaphezu kwama-70. Ukusuka kwindawo yethu eSalt River, sikhathalela iintsapho kwiCity Bowl, kwiiNtshona zeMazantsi naseMantla, nakwiNtshona Koloni yonke. Iqela lethu elinamava libonelela ngenkxaso eyenzelwe wena — enoba ufuna uncedo olungxamisekileyo okanye ucwangcisa kwangaphambili.

Izithuthi zethu

Uthutho olunesidima, olugcinwe lucocekile.

Ihesi yethu yakudala ithwele iintsapho zaseKapa kangangamashumi eminyaka. Ipholishwa, iservisiwe kwaye ikhangelwe phambi kwenkonzo nganye, kuba uhambo oluya engcwabeni lufanele ukhathalelo olufanayo nako konke esikwenzayo.

Iimpendulo ezicacileyo, ezicaciswa ngobubele

Imibuzo iintsapho ezidla ngokuyibuza.

Akufuneki ukuba uqonde yonke into phambi kokuba ushaye ucingo. Iqela lethu licacisa inyathelo ngenyathelo likuncede wenze isigqibo esinye ngexesha.

01

Kutheni ukhethe ukutshiswa eKapa?

Ukutshiswa yindlela engabizi kakhulu enceda kwingxaki yokunqongophala kwendawo yokungcwaba eKapa. ISixeko sikhuthaza iintsapho ukuba zicinge ngokutshiswa apho kuhambelana neenkolelo zabo. Sinikezela ngokutshiswa ngentlonipho, kunye neenketho zesikhumbuzo nokusasaza uthuthu.

02

Ngawaphi amaxwebhu afunekayo?

Siyawaqhuba amaxwebhu egameni lakho. Ngokuqhelekileyo kufuneka isatifikethi sogqirha sonobangela wokusweleka (BI-1663), isazisi kunye needilesi zalowo oswelekileyo. Iqela lethu liya kukucebisa ngamanye amaxwebhu afunekayo.

03

Ninikezela ngeenketho ezikhusela indalo?

Ewe. Singalungiselela imingcwabo ekhusela indalo esebenzisa iibhokisi zeplanga size siphephe intsimbi okanye iglu yeekhemikhali, njengoko kucetyiswa yiSixeko saseKapa.

04

Ndingafumana phi inkxaso yeemvakalelo?

Ukulahlekelwa kunokuba nzima kakhulu. Ukufumana uncedo oluyimfihlo, tsalela iLifeLine South Africa ku-0861 322 322, i-South African Depression & Anxiety Group (0800 567 567) okanye iNetcare Akeso Crisis Line (0861 435 787). Nathi silapha ukukuxhasa.
