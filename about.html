Our story

Founded in 1943 by William Sydney Engledoe, our funeral home has been a trusted presence in the heart of Cape Town for generations. What started as a small parlour in the City Bowl has grown into a family-run business serving communities across the Western Cape. Across eight decades, our family has had the profound honour of guiding more than 10 000 families through their most difficult moments — and we have remained committed throughout to compassionate, personalised care.

Heritage in the Western Cape

From the bustling streets of District Six to the serene vineyards of the Winelands, we have walked alongside families from every corner of our province. Our experience spans diverse cultures and traditions, and we honour each service with sensitivity and respect. Many Cape Townians know us for our attentive guidance during some of life’s most challenging moments.

Looking ahead

As our city grows and changes, so do we. We continue to invest in training, facilities and technology to provide respectful, modern services while staying true to our roots. Thank you for trusting WS Engledoe & Sons with your family’s final farewells.

What guides every arrangement

Our values are not a poster on the wall.

They are the reason families return to us across generations, and the standard we hold ourselves to on the hardest days.

Compassion

We approach every family with empathy, understanding that grief is deeply personal and never runs to a schedule.

Integrity

Transparency and honesty underpin everything we do, from the first quote through to service delivery.

Tradition & innovation

We respect long-held customs while embracing newer approaches such as eco-friendly funerals and digital memorials.

Local service

As Cape Town locals we understand our communities and work with suppliers and venues we know personally.
