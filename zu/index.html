Igama lomndeni elakhelwe enkonzweni

Ifa lokunakekela kusukela ngo-1955.

Njengebhizinisi lomndeni elinesisekelo eKapa, sihole imindeni ngobubele iminyaka engaphezu kwengu-70. Kusukela endaweni yethu eSalt River, sinakekela imindeni eseCity Bowl, emadolobheni aseningizimu nasenyakatho, nakulo lonke iNtshonalanga Kapa. Ithimba lethu elinolwazi linikeza ukwesekwa komuntu siqu — kungakhathaliseki ukuthi udinga usizo oluphuthumayo noma uhlela kusengaphambili.

Izimpendulo ezicacile, ezichazwa ngomusa

Imibuzo imindeni evame ukuyibuza.

Awudingi ukuqonda yonke imininingwane ngaphambi kokushayela. Ithimba lethu lichaza isinyathelo ngesinyathelo likusize wenze isinqumo esisodwa ngesikhathi.

01

Kungani ukhethe ukushiswa eKapa?

Ukushiswa kuyindlela engabizi kakhulu esiza ekunciphiseni ukushoda kwezindawo zokungcwaba eKapa. IDolobha likhuthaza imindeni ukuthi icabangele ukushiswa lapho kuhambisana nezinkolelo zayo. Sihlinzeka ngokushiswa ngokuhlonipha, kanye nezinketho zesikhumbuzo nokusabalalisa umlotha.

02

Yimiphi imibhalo edingekayo?

Siyayiphatha imibhalo egameni lakho. Ngokuvamile udinga isitifiketi sikadokotela sembangela yokushona (BI-1663), kanye nomazisi nemininingwane yekheli lomufi. Ithimba lethu lizokweluleka ngeminye imibhalo edingekayo.

03

Ninikeza izinketho ezinakekela imvelo?

Yebo. Singahlela imingcwabo enakekela imvelo ngokusebenzisa amabhokisi okhuni futhi sigweme insimbi noma uglue wamakhemikhali, njengokunconywa yiDolobha laseKapa.

04

Ngingathola kuphi ukwesekwa ngokomzwelo?

Ukulahlekelwa kungakhathaza kakhulu. Ukuthola usizo oluyimfihlo, shayela iLifeLine South Africa ku-0861 322 322, i-South African Depression & Anxiety Group (0800 567 567) noma iNetcare Akeso Crisis Line (0861 435 787). Nathi silapha ukukusekela.

Imigqa yethu ivulekile amahora angu-24 ukunikeza usizo olusheshayo, izimpendulo ngezinqubomgomo noma ingxoxo enomusa mayelana nokuhlela kusengaphambili.
