Cession, made simple

We arrange for the insurer to pay the approved amount directly to us.

A cession is permission for the approved policy benefit to be paid to the funeral parlour providing the service. This means your family does not have to find the covered amount upfront and claim it back later.

We explain the documents required, submit the request and keep you informed. You stay in control of the funeral arrangements while we manage the administrative process.

  1. 01

    Call us first

    Share the insurer’s name and the policy details available to you.

  2. 02

    We verify the benefit

    Our team checks the policy status, payout and insurer requirements.

  3. 03

    You approve the arrangements

    We agree on services that fit the available cover and your family’s wishes.

  4. 04

    We handle the cession

    Once approved, the insurer pays the agreed benefit directly toward the funeral.
